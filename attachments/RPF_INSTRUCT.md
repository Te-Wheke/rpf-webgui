PROPAGATION

For every material claim:

1. Propagate FORWARD from the claim towards reality and evidence.
2. Propagate REVERSE from the conclusion towards its originating problem, evidence, assumptions and dependencies.

SIX OPERATORS

At every key or dependency systematically apply:

WHAT | HOW | WHY | WHO | WHERE | WHEN

These are dimensions of interrogation, not merely questions. Apply them recursively wherever a material dependency requires examination.

FORWARD PROPAGATION

CLAIM
→ identify keys
→ interrogate keys
→ establish evidence
→ establish dependencies
→ distinguish observation from inference
→ identify unknowns
→ identify contradictions
→ determine what can be established about reality
→ formulate conclusion

REVERSE PROPAGATION

CONCLUSION
→ identify required dependencies
→ trace dependencies backwards
→ identify originating evidence/observation
→ identify originating problem/question
→ expose unsupported assumptions
→ identify unresolved dependencies
→ identify next proof/audit target

Reverse propagation must produce actionable outcomes, including the remaining uncertainty and next audit target.

KNOWLEDGE PROGRESSION

Reality

That which exists independently of perception, opinion, language or belief.

Observation

Conscious interaction with reality.

Experience

Repeated observation and participation producing patterns.

Understanding

Recognition of relationships, causes and consequences.

Language

The means by which understanding is preserved, communicated and refined across individuals and generations.

Knowledge

Organised understanding capable of being communicated, tested, preserved and improved.

Wisdom

Demonstrated understanding of why knowledge succeeds, fails or ought to be applied.

Stewardship

Responsible application, preservation, monitoring, revision and transmission of wisdom for present and future generations.

NON-SKIPPING RULE

Do not treat a conclusion as sufficiently established when material dependencies remain unresolved.

Do not:

- represent assumptions as facts;
- represent inference as observation;
- manufacture evidence;
- manufacture provenance;
- manufacture certainty;
- manufacture sources;
- silently resolve contradictions;
- conceal unknowns.

INCOMPLETENESS

RPF permits incomplete conclusions.

When material uncertainty remains, explicitly identify the conclusion as incomplete rather than forcing a binary valid/invalid determination. The framework recognises UNRESOLVED as a legitimate claim state.

EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

For every material assertion distinguish:

FACT / EVIDENCE
INFERENCE
ASSUMPTION
PROPOSAL
UNKNOWN

If evidence is absent, identify what evidence would be required to establish the claim.

STEWARDSHIP TEST

Stewardship is justified only when sufficient understanding exists to determine:

WHAT should be applied
WHY it should be applied
HOW it should be applied
WHO holds responsibility
WHERE it applies
WHEN it applies
LIMITS
FAILURE CONDITIONS
MONITORING
PRESERVATION
REVISION
TRANSMISSION

Stewardship is therefore not merely possession or maintenance. It is the responsible operational continuation of demonstrated understanding.

PRIMARY PRINCIPLE

«Propagate every material claim from claim to reality, and every conclusion back through its dependencies, until the evidence, reasoning, uncertainty and stewardship conditions are explicit.»

RPF is a repeatable methodology for disciplined inquiry that can be taught, repeated, audited, compared and improved.

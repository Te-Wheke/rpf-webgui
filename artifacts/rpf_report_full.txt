                               RPF Investigation Desk
                         Source Analysis and Integrity Review
Prepared for: Grok development review | Date: 22 November 2026

Scope: Static review of supplied workspace, focusing on the RPF engine, evidence admission, dependency enforcement,
state transitions, and separation from SCA / T-Lab.

Assessment: Functional deterministic prototype; core justification assurances are not yet fully enforced.


1. Executive assessment
The supplied TypeScript workspace implements an eight-stage RPF investigation model, proposition-level claim states,
domain-sensitive evidence standards, six operators, forward evaluation, reverse audit targeting, seeded cases, and
browser-based persistence. The engine is rule-based rather than a mere language-model narration. However, important
rules depend on self-declared evidence metadata and do not establish source authenticity, universal relevance, or a
complete chain of record-level dependencies.

Central conclusion: The design expresses the principle 'No transition should occur unless justified by the dependencies
beneath it,' but the inspected code does not fully enforce that principle. Do not present the prototype as a verified
independent evidence authority.

4. Findings and remediation priorities
RPF-001 | HIGH | Evidence history is not immutable

Evidence is persisted through browser localStorage (store.ts). The reviewed evidence update path can replace a matching
identifier, so 'append-only' is not an enforceable storage property. Use an append-only event log with immutable IDs, explicit
correction events, linked hashes, and independent verification.

RPF-002 | HIGH | Evidence classifications are self-asserted

EvidenceDraft accepts kind, standard, provenance, and optional integrity metadata. The evaluation logic can promote
evidence labelled FACT when procedural fields meet rules, without independent authentication. Separate submitter
assertions from verified admissions and require provenance verification appropriate to the domain.

RPF-004 | MEDIUM-HIGH | Cross-stage evidence dependencies are weak

Experience readiness checks observer, timestamp and provenance, but does not universally bind an experience to the
observation that supports the same proposition. Require typed dependency edges and enforce each stage against the
specific preceding record.

RPF-005 | HIGH | Stewardship can bypass a Wisdom check

testStewardship() explicitly checks Knowledge and text fields, but does not explicitly require a justified Wisdom stage.
Require all prerequisite stages, including Wisdom, and validate the substance of the proposed action and its constraints.

RPF-006 | MEDIUM-HIGH | Hashing is not authentication

SHA-256 provides a digest, not proof that a source or observation is genuine. Verify hashes against trusted records;
preserve original bytes, trusted timestamps, signer identities where applicable, and a clear provenance chain.


5. Architectural boundaries
RPF: Investigation and justification assessment only. SCA: Authorised execution and orchestration. T-Lab: Model
engineering, laboratory evaluation, and experimental artefact production. Data should cross these boundaries as
characterised artefacts; neither an SCA result nor a T-Lab trace automatically becomes a verified observation. Synthetic CSI
data must remain clearly identified and inadmissible as real-world measurement.


6. Recommended integrity work package
1. Implement immutable event-sourced evidence records, signed or otherwise independently anchored where warranted.

2. Define evidence admission statuses distinct from FACT / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION, with source authenticity and
integrity checks.

3. Replace permissive relevance fallback with explicit proposition-linked relevance decisions and reasons.

4. Require record-level dependency edges across Reality, Observation, Experience, Understanding, Language, Knowledge,
Wisdom, Stewardship.

5. Gate Stewardship on every required stage, including Wisdom, and on domain-appropriate action validation.

6. Add negative tests: forged provenance, unrelated FACT, record replacement, stage bypass, synthetic CSI promotion,
conflicting evidence, and tampered exports.

7. Publish a machine-readable decision trace: input record IDs, rule versions, reasons, gaps, contradictions, and export
checksum.


7. Verification criteria for Grok
A corrective build should show: (a) original evidence remains retrievable after amendments; (b) unverifiable or irrelevant
material cannot advance claims; (c) stage dependencies are explicit and traversable; (d) Wisdom cannot be bypassed
before Stewardship; (e) exports can be independently validated; and (f) regression tests cover adversarial inputs, not only
expected seed scenarios.


8. Final disposition
Disposition: INVESTIGATE / REMEDIATE, not operational certification. The prototype is suitable for continued
controlled development. It is not yet justified to claim that its evidence ledger is immutable, that evidence authenticity is
verified, or that every stage transition is dependency-safe. Preserve the non-executing RPF boundary and address integrity
controls before extending integration or presentation claims.
